SLA Business Logic
1. Clock Start: SLA clock starts immediately at ticket creation (created_at).
2. On-Hold Pausing: Placing a request into ON_HOLD pauses SLA countdown. Paused time is dynamically added to total_on_hold_seconds, extending the due date.
3. Priority Shifts: Changing priority recalculates the SLA target and adjusts the due date.
4. Warning & Breach: Tickets reaching ≥75% duration enter APPROACHING_BREACH. Past 100% they are flagged as SLA_BREACHED.
